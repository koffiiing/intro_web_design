Chicago-Style Hot Dog Recipe

A Chicago-Style Hot Dog is the single greatest way to eat a hot dog. All other ways are inferier.
The Chicago-Style Hot Dog is loaded with pickles, tomatoes, onion, sweet relish, black pepper, pickled peppers, yellow mustard, and celery salt!

Prep time: 10 mins
Cooking time: 5 mins
Servings: 4

    Ingredients
  • All Beef Nathan's Hot Dogs
  • Yellow Mustard
  • Black Pepper
  • Pickles
