Chicago-Style Hot Dog Recipe

A Chicago-Style Hot Dog is the single greatest way to eat a hot dog. All other ways are inferior. The Chicago-Style Hot Dog is loaded with pickles, tomatoes, onion, sweet relish, black pepper, pickled peppers, yellow mustard, and celery salt! Just don't you dare put ketchup on it.

Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 5 minutes
Total Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 4 hot dogs

Ingredients

  • 4 Nathan's all-beef hot dogs
  • 4 sesame seed hot dog buns
  • 4 teaspoons yellow mustard
  • 1/4 cup sweet pickle relish
  • 1/4 cup diced white onion
  • 1 medium tomato, cut into 8 wedges
  • 4 dill pickle spears
  • 8 pickled sport peppers
  • 1/2 teaspoon celery salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Habanero hot sauce, to taste (optional)

Directions

  1. Get your toppings ready first: dice the onion, cut the tomato into 8 wedges, and set out the mustard, relish, pickle spears, and pickled peppers.
  2. Fill a medium pot about halfway with water and bring it to a boil over high heat.
  3. Turn the heat down to medium-low so the water is just simmering. Add the hot dogs and let them cook for 5 minutes, until they are hot all the way through.
  4. Use tongs to take the hot dogs out of the water and put each one in a bun.
  5. Squeeze a zigzag of yellow mustard (about 1 teaspoon) down each hot dog, then add 1 tablespoon of relish and 1 tablespoon of diced onion.
  6. Tuck 2 tomato wedges along one side of each hot dog and a pickle spear along the other side. Place 2 pickled peppers on top.
  7. Finish each hot dog with a pinch of celery salt and a dash of black pepper. Add a few drops of habanero hot sauce if you want some extra heat.
  8. Admire its beauty for a moment, then eat it, gosh dang it!

What Goes Well With It?

Potato Chips
Salty, crunchy chips are a great contrast to the soft bun and juicy toppings.
French Fries
Chicago hot dog stands often serve their dogs with a pile of fries, so this is the classic side.
Ice-Cold Coca-Cola
A nice, cold Coke in a can is sweet and fizzy, which cools things down after all the tangy pickles and peppers.
Habanero Hot Sauce
A few drops give it a little kick, and the fruity habanero flavor goes great with the sweet relish.
