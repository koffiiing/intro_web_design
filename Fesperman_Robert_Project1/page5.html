So Which Workout Should You Choose?

The good news is that both workouts work. The study's authors say people who want a fun, social, skill-based activity may prefer handball games, while people who want the most efficient way to improve their heart and lung fitness may prefer HIIT (Han et al., 2026). Other research has found that small-sided games are more enjoyable than HIIT at a similar intensity (Selmi et al., 2020), and enjoying exercise makes it easier to stick with.

Choose SSG if you... Choose HIIT if you...
Like team sports and playing with friends Prefer to work out on your own
Get bored easily with regular workouts Like a clear plan with exact times
Want to build sports skills too Want the biggest boost to aerobic fitness
Have a gym or court and a group to play with Only have a track or open space

Tips for Getting Started

  • Start slowly and build up over a few weeks.
  • Always warm up before hard exercise.
  • Work with a coach or trainer if you are new to high-intensity exercise.
  • Talk to a doctor first if you have a health condition.
  • Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity each week (Bull et al., 2020).

Things to Keep in Mind

This study only included young, healthy college students, and it did not check back with them after the 16 weeks ended. The researchers say future studies should include older adults and people at higher risk of health problems, and should follow people for longer (Han et al., 2026).
