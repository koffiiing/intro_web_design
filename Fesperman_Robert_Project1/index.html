Welcome!


Team handball is fast, social, and a great workout.

More than one-third of adults around the world do not get the minimum amount of exercise recommended by the World Health Organization, which is 150 minutes of moderate activity per week (Bull et al., 2020). Sitting too much is linked to obesity, type 2 diabetes, and heart disease. The problem is that a lot of people find regular workouts boring and quit.

This website looks at a 2026 research study that asked a simple question: is playing a fun team game just as good for your health as a tough, structured running workout? Researchers compared small-sided handball games (SSG) with high-intensity interval training (HIIT) in 90 college students who had not been exercising. After 16 weeks, both groups got healthier and fitter (Han et al., 2026).

Key Points at a Glance

  • Both handball games and HIIT lowered body weight, BMI, blood pressure, and resting heart rate.
  • Both groups improved their aerobic fitness and leg strength much more than people who did not train.
  • For most results there was no real difference between the two workouts.
  • HIIT gave a slightly bigger boost to aerobic fitness, while handball was more social and game-like.
